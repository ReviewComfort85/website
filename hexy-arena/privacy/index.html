Hexy Arena — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Hexy Arena ("the game", "we", "us") is a casual online multiplayer game made for players of all ages, including children. We collect as little data as possible, we do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and how you can delete it.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (Firebase Analytics is turned off). No advertising ID is collected.
  • No personalised advertising and no ads in the game today. No selling or renting of data.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access.
  • No real names, photos or emails on leaderboards or in matches.

If optional rewarded ads are ever added, they will be contextual and non-personalised only, served by a provider certified for Google Play's Families programme, and this policy will be updated before they appear.

Children

Hexy Arena is designed to be safe for children. Under the GDPR (Art. 8) and Bulgarian law, children under 14 need a parent's consent for some online services; that is why we built the game so that it does not depend on a child's personal data:

  • We do not ask for a name, email, phone number or password. The player ID is random. Only if a player chooses to link a Google account does Firebase Authentication store the email and name from Google, for sign-in and saving progress only (see Optional Google sign-in).
  • We do not use analytics, profiling or personalised ads, and we do not share children's data with third parties for marketing.
  • The only thing other players can see is the nickname, trophies, league, level and skins.
  • Every purchase is protected by a parent check in the game and by the Google Play purchase confirmation.
  • Parents can delete their child's account in the game or ask us by email (see below), and can contact us with any question.

Purchases and the Hexy Pass

You can buy the Hexy Pass (a season pass with extra cosmetic rewards), skins, a starter pack and other items with real money through Google Play. Coins cannot be bought. All purchases are cosmetic and give no advantage in matches. We receive only the information listed in the table above, so we can confirm and restore your purchases.

Services we use (processors)

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Remote Config). The database is stored in the EU (eur3 multi-region). Firebase privacy
  • Oracle Cloud, Frankfurt, Germany: our game server that runs online matches and the game economy.
  • Cloudflare: secure connection between the app and our server. Cloudflare privacy
  • Google Play (and later the Apple App Store) for payments and purchase verification.

These companies process data only on our instructions. Where data may be processed outside the EU/EEA (for example by Google or Cloudflare), this is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: as long as your account exists. Season history is kept so your past league results stay on your profile.
  • Inactive guest accounts may be removed after 24 months without play.
  • After you delete your account, the data is removed from our live systems at once and disappears from our server backups within 7 days.
  • Purchase records needed for accounting are kept only as long as Bulgarian accounting and tax law requires, without game data attached.

How to delete your account and data

  • In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account. This immediately removes your player, nickname, progress, friends and Google link from our servers.
  • Without the app: email info@reviewcomfort85.com with the subject "Hexy Arena account deletion" and your player nickname or friend code (shown in the game under Profile). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting the account does not cancel purchases made through Google Play; refunds follow Google Play's rules.

Your rights (GDPR)

You (or your parent) have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to receive a copy of it (portability). Write to info@reviewcomfort85.com; we answer within one month. You may also complain to a data protection authority; in Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg).

Changes

We will update this page when anything changes and show the new date at the top. Important changes will also be announced in the game.
